# Code of Conduct

## The short version

Be decent to people. Assume good faith. Argue about code, not about each other.

## What's expected

- Give feedback on the work, not the person. "This will deadlock under X" is useful;
  "this is amateur" is not.
- Assume the other person knows something you don't. They usually do.
- Accept that maintainers say no to things. A closed issue is a scoping decision, not a
  judgement of you.
- Remember that most people here are not writing in their first language. Read for intent.

## What isn't

Harassment, personal attacks, discriminatory remarks, sustained disruption, or publishing
someone's private information.

## Reporting

Email **tkakashofficial@gmail.com**. Reports are handled privately. I will respond within a few
days, and I won't share your report with the person it concerns without telling you first.

Responses range from a private word to a permanent ban, depending on severity and pattern.

## Scope

Applies in this repository's issues, pull requests, and discussions, and anywhere someone is
representing the project.
